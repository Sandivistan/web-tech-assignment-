About and contact

What the portal does and who to ask for help.

What the portal is for

The Smart Campus Events Portal brings XYZ University's events together in one place, so students do not have to search noticeboards, emails and club pages.

With the portal you can:

  • see upcoming events with their date, time and venue;
  • search events and filter them by category;
  • register for an event and see your saved registrations.

Frequently asked questions

How do I register for an event?

Open the Events page, choose an event and select Register. The event is already chosen in the form, so you only need to add your details.

Where are my registrations saved?

They are saved in this browser on this computer. They are not sent to the events office, and you will not see them in another browser.

How do I remove my registrations?

Go to the Register page and select Clear registrations. This removes all of them from this browser.

What does a student ID look like?

It is one letter followed by eight digits, for example S12345678.

Event terms

  • Give your own, correct details when you register.
  • Bring your student card to the event.
  • Follow the instructions of the event staff.
  • Contact the events office if you can no longer attend.
